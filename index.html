Research

Reliable models, optimized decisions, large data.

My work connects three threads: testing ML software behavior, learning from optimization, and modeling complex systems with large-scale data.

ML Framework Reliability

Studying TensorFlow and PyTorch behavior under edge cases, including operator behavior, errors, CPU/GPU execution, and model outputs.

Learning-Augmented Optimization

Using Gurobi-generated MILP solutions as training targets so neural models can learn from exact optimization and support faster dispatch decisions.

Renewable Energy Dispatch

Working with hybrid wind farms and energy storage: when to send power to the grid, when to store it, and when to discharge later.

Graph and Data Systems

Modeling complex systems with knowledge graphs, large-scale data pipelines, graph algorithms, and AI-assisted prediction.

Wrestling

Combat sports built the way I work.

Wrestling is where I learned discipline in its most practical form: repeat the hard thing, stay calm under pressure, and keep improving even when nobody sees the work. I competed in varsity high school wrestling, club college wrestling, and independent training with C2X. I carry that mentality into my research. Wrestling is arguably one of the toughest sports on the mind. Thankfully, I never had to do drastic weight cuts, however, I know plenty of people who did. It is very easy to break down or burn out which is a common theme that high achieving individuals all fear. Burn out is looming for individuals that see their job or sport as a tough obstacle. I love the sport as much as I love academia. This is why I believe that I will not get exhaustive enough to quit. Never once had it or will cross my mind.

I also have a strong jiu-jitsu background, that adds another layer. The same sport that made me think about leverage, timing, sequences, and constraints, feels close to how I think about technical systems.

Hiking and travel

Mountains and nature

I like hiking, especially high terrain, because it helps me escape screen time and get a good workout. I have hiked in the Krkonose Mountains near Czech Republic, the Alps around Slovenia, and the Andes, and those trips made climate, terrain, weather, and energy feel less abstract.

Being in places like that changes how I think about the environment. This is why I am so passionate about climate change because I first-hand feel the effects of global warming when I notice the summers hotter and winters milder. I strongly believe that we can help increase renwable energy and decrease our carbon footprint by using smaller AI models in order to achieve this. Distance, altitude, weather, water, and risk are not just variables in the model, but realties.
